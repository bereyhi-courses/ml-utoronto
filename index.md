## Final Project Submission and Presentation

### Presentation Format

This year, we adapt an alternative presentation scheme which makes more flexibility. The procedure is as follows:
- Each couple of groups have been assigned to a cluster with supervised by a TA. Please see the clusters here on [Quercus.](https://q.utoronto.ca/courses/419528)
- For each cluster a time window of 2 hours on __April 6__ is dedicated.
- The groups will show up during that time window and will present their project to the TA and Instructor (a Hackathon style).
- Each group will present for 5 minutes and will be asked questions for 5 minutes.

Please check logistic details on [Quercus.](https://q.utoronto.ca/courses/419528)

 
### Final Report and Code Submission
The final report and source code are due on __April 12.__ Please note that
- Your report must meet the template and page limit.
- You may (and strongly recommended) to reflect the feedback you get during the presentation in your final report.
